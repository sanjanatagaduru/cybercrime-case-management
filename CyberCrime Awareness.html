Cybercrime Awareness

🔹 What is Cybercrime?

Cybercrime refers to illegal activities carried out using computers, mobile devices, or the internet. These crimes target personal data, money, and identity.

🔹 Types of Cybercrimes

  • Phishing attacks (fake emails or messages)
  • Online fraud and payment scams
  • Identity theft
  • Social media account hacking
  • Fake job or lottery scams
  • Malware and virus attacks

🔹 Warning Signs of Cyber Attacks

  • Receiving unknown OTPs without requesting
  • Messages asking for personal or bank details
  • Suspicious links or fake websites
  • Unauthorized login alerts
  • Money deducted without your permission
  • Emails or messages sent from your account without your knowledge

Never share your OTP, password, or bank details with anyone.

🔹 Precautions to Avoid Cybercrime

  • Use strong passwords and change them regularly
  • Do not click on unknown links
  • Verify websites before entering details
  • Enable two-factor authentication
  • Avoid using public Wi-Fi for transactions
  • Keep your software and antivirus updated

🔹 What to Do if You Are Attacked?

  • Do not panic
  • Immediately change your passwords
  • Block your bank account if money is involved
  • Report the incident through cybercrime report system
  • Inform the concerned authorities
